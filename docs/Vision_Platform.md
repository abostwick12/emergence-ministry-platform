# Vision Platform

## Definition

The Vision Platform is the future church-level decision-support environment built on top of operational data, Meridian memory, evidence-linked signals, and human-reviewed insights.

Lead Emergence is the first operational strand. Meridian is the memory layer. The Vision Platform is the broader leadership surface that can eventually compare ministry direction, capacity, outcomes, resources, and decisions across the church.

## Principles

- Human leadership remains primary.
- Evidence must be visible.
- AI language must stay cautious.
- Sensitive data boundaries are architectural, not merely prompt instructions.
- Strategic views should link back to operational workspaces where action happens.

## Future Capabilities

- cross-ministry decision centers
- church-level goals and initiatives
- approved knowledge retrieval across ministries
- resource and teaching alignment
- volunteer and staff capacity review
- long-range planning scenarios
- leadership decision history

## Deferred Until After Competition

- predictive staffing
- autonomous recommendations
- cross-ministry ontology
- executive dashboards
- two-way Meridian sync
- church-wide Meridian Web
