# Content Interview Playbook

Offer **Start guided interview** and **Skip interview** side by side. When guided, ask one question at a time. Select the next dimension from what remains unresolved in the user's answers: purpose, audience, concrete substance, response, tone, and visual direction. Prioritize visual direction for Instagram and church slides and practical response for GroupMe and event promotion.

Follow an answer only when more specificity will materially improve the draft. Do not march through a fixed questionnaire. Stop when purpose, audience, substance, and response are adequately covered; when the user asks to finish; or after six answers, whichever comes first.
