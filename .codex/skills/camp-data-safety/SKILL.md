# Camp Data Safety

## Purpose

Keep sensitive camper data confined to its proper place while Camp operational features are built out.

## Rules

- General roster, team, transportation, and leader views avoid detailed medication, allergy, insurance, guardian-contact, physician, and medical-note data.
- Use neutral, safe indicators outside Medical Command instead of exposing the underlying sensitive detail.
- Preserve the existing medication audit and correction behavior.
- Do not let safety checks become a reason normal Camp workflows remain unbuilt.
